The current jevailab workspace creates still images from a published template and a text description. This guide explains the workflow and how to prepare a useful prompt.

## 1. Choose a template

Open the image workspace and choose a visual direction from the published templates. Each template provides a starting style for the result.

If you want to begin without a template, choose **Free creation** and describe the image you want.

## 2. Describe the image

Write a short, specific description. Useful details include:

- the main subject and action;
- the setting and time of day;
- the lighting, colors, and mood;
- the composition or visual style you want.

For example: *A quiet coastal breakfast scene at sunrise, soft warm light, pale blue and cream colors, airy editorial composition.*

## 3. Choose an image ratio

Select the output ratio that fits where you plan to use the image. The workspace shows the active ratio before generation.

## 4. Review and generate

Accept the content-safety confirmation and review the credit quote shown before submission. The server-calculated quote is the source of truth. After the image is ready, use the download control in the preview area.

## A note on accuracy

Only image generation is available in this workspace. Published descriptions describe that current workflow; features shown as unavailable are not part of the live service.
